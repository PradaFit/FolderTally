===================================================
NOTES

TXT is the default. For the other formats:
py FolderTally.py "D:\Games" -f txt
py FolderTally.py "D:\Games" -f json
py FolderTally.py "D:\Games" -f pdf

Custom output location:

py FolderTally.py "D:\Games" -f json -o "D:\Reports\games.json"

If a custom output already exists:

py FolderTally.py "D:\Games" -f json -o "D:\Reports\games.json" --overwrite
