Collected license texts are in the portable folder's `licenses` directory and are available in About. The original source archives also retain their notices and licenses, including those for development tools not shipped as runtime binaries. The application does not download fonts or dependencies while running.

Build and audit utilities are development tools. They are not application services. Windows system components remain subject to their own licenses.

The `sources` directory supplies the unmodified Qt Base and PySide/Shiboken 6.11.2 upstream source archives, including their build scripts. `sources/qt-sources.json` records upstream URLs and SHA-256 hashes. FolderTally makes no changes to those libraries. See `LIBRARY_REPLACEMENT.md` in the portable folder, or [the repository copy](packaging/LIBRARY_REPLACEMENT.md), for replacement and rebuilding instructions. You may modify or replace the LGPL libraries and reverse engineer the application as needed to debug those modifications, without an application-license restriction on those rights.
